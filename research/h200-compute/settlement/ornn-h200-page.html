What is the price of H200 per hour?

NVIDIA H200 rental compute settles at $4.63 per GPU-hour on the Ornn Compute Price Index (OCPI-H200), as of September 4, 2026. That is $111.01 per GPU-day and roughly $3330.30 per GPU-month at current levels. The value settles once per trading day by 20:00 UTC; regional and term pricing varies around it.

Is H200 getting cheaper or more expensive?

The OCPI-H200 has moved +3.0% over the last seven days and -3.8% over thirty days (from $4.81 per GPU-hour). The three-month public window spans $2.99 (low) to $5.62 (high). Daily settled values and 1/7/30-day deltas are published on this page.

Where can I rent H200 GPUs?

Ornn Compute (compute.ornn.com) reserves dedicated NVIDIA H200 capacity across verified neocloud providers, priced against the same settled OCPI-H200 index published on this page, with access in minutes. Reservations can be resold on a secondary market if requirements change, so unused capacity is recoverable rather than sunk. Ornn both prices compute and rents it.

How is the OCPI-H200 calculated?

The OCPI-H200 is a volume-weighted, winsorized average of transacted H200 rental prices across the neocloud market — real trades between buyers and sellers, not scraped listings, surveys, or provider rate cards. It is the settled benchmark used for pricing, hedging, and settlement in the compute market, and it is listed on Bloomberg. The universe, calculation, governance, and revision policy are published in full in the OCPI methodology.
